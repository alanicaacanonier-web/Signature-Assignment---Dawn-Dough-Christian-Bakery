Frequently Asked Questions

?How far in advance should I place orders?

Menu orders can usually be placed at least 24 hours in advance. Custom cakes, bulk orders, and event dessert tables should be requested 5–7 days ahead so there is enough time to confirm details.

?Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery is available for local orders within the service area. A small delivery fee may be added based on distance and order size.

?Can I request custom items?

Yes. Customers can request custom cakes, dessert trays, faith-themed baked goods, and bulk orders through the custom order form.

?Are there allergy-friendly options?

Some allergy-conscious options may be available by request. Customers should list allergies clearly so the bakery can discuss safe preparation options.
